# Design Mastery Skill

Injects premium design principles into generated components.

## Core Directives

1. **Dieter Rams' 10 Principles of Good Design**: Keep designs honest, useful, aesthetically pleasing, long-lasting, and thorough down to the last detail.
2. **Visual Hierarchy**: Use contrast in weight, scale, and color to clearly separate headlines, subheadings, and body content. Avoid uniform layouts.
3. **Typography**: Use modern typeface pairings (e.g., sans-serif geometric headers with clean body fonts) and strict line-height scales (1.2x for headings, 1.5x for body).
4. **Grids & Layouts**: Align elements strictly to columns and rows. Use asymmetry, negative space, and visual focal points to establish a premium feel.
